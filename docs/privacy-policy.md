# Privacy Policy - Minimal YouTube

Last updated: November 10, 2026

## What the extension does with your data
Minimal YouTube does not collect, store, or sell personal data, and the developer receives no data from the extension.

## What leaves your device
While you type in the search box on the Focus mode page, the text you type is sent to Google (`suggestqueries.google.com`) to show autocomplete suggestions. Google also receives your IP address with that request, like any web request. The extension does not store this text. Google's handling of it is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Nothing is sent when you are not typing in that box.

## Settings
The extension saves one setting on your device (whether Focus mode is on) in Chrome's local extension storage. It never leaves your device.

## Limited Use
Data the extension handles is used only to provide autocomplete suggestions. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to build profiles.

## Not affiliated
Minimal YouTube is an independent extension. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
