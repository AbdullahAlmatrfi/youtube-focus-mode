Privacy Policy - Minimal YouTube

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Overview

Minimal YouTube does not collect, store, or sell any personal data.

Data Collection

The extension does not collect user data, browsing history, or personally identifiable information.

Third-Party Services

The extension fetches search suggestions from suggestqueries.google.com to provide autocomplete while typing. No user-identifying data is stored by the extension.
